Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Add your chosen fragrances to your cart, then go to checkout and enter your delivery details. You will receive an order summary to send to us by email or WhatsApp, and we will confirm your order with you.

How do I choose a size?

Most fragrances come in 30ml, 50ml and 100ml. Luxury extraits come in 50ml and 100ml. Choose your size on the product page.

What is the difference between eau de parfum and extrait?

Extrait de parfum is the most concentrated form, with a higher proportion of fragrance oil. It is richer and more intense, so a little goes a long way.

Can I buy a fragrance as a gift?

Yes. Our gift sets are designed for giving, from a four-scent discovery set to a signature trio.
